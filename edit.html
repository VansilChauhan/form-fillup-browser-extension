Tips for drop-downs, checkboxes, radios and dates
  • Drop-downs and radio buttons: type the option as it appears on screen, e.g. United States.
  • Checkboxes: yes to tick, no to untick.
  • Dates: 2025-03-14, 14 March 2025 or March 14, 2025. Avoid 03/04/2025; it's ambiguous and date pickers will skip it. Plain text date boxes get your value exactly as typed.
  • Different labels on different sites: add another row with the same value, e.g. both "Zip" and "Postal code".
